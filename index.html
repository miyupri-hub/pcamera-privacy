PCamera Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

PCamera is a video recorder. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone.

Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only while you are recording. Audio can be turned off in settings, or muted during a recording. The app does not watch or listen when you are not recording.

Videos

Recordings are stored on your device. If app lock is on, they are encrypted on the device. The app does not upload your videos to the developer. If you turn on “Also save a copy”, a copy is written to your gallery or to a folder you choose. A video you delete in the app is kept in the in-app trash for 14 days, then removed automatically, unless you restore it or delete it permanently sooner. Emptying the trash or uninstalling the app removes the in-app copy. A copy you saved to the gallery or another folder stays until you delete it yourself.

Advertising

If you have not bought “Remove ads”, the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use the advertising ID and similar device data to show and measure ads. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. The app does not receive your card number or bank details. It only learns whether the “Remove ads” purchase is owned on this Google account.

What we do not collect

There is no PCamera account. The developer does not run a server that receives your videos, password, or contacts.

Children

PCamera is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer email address shown on the PCamera page on Google Play.

私隱政策（中文）

PCamera 是一部錄影應用程式。本政策說明應用程式如何處理你手機上的資料。

鏡頭與麥克風

鏡頭與麥克風只會在你錄影時使用。你可以在設定中關閉錄音，或在錄影中靜音。你沒有在錄影時，應用程式不會開啟鏡頭或麥克風。

影片

錄影儲存在你的裝置上。開啟應用程式鎖後，應用程式內的影片會在裝置上加密。開發者不會收到你的影片。若你開啟「另外儲存一份」，系統會把一份副本寫入相簿，或你選擇的資料夾。在應用程式內刪除的影片會在垃圾桶保留 14 日，然後自動移除；你也可以提早還原或永久刪除。清空垃圾桶或解除安裝應用程式，會移除應用程式內的副本。已儲存到相簿或其他資料夾的副本，須由你自行刪除。

廣告

若你尚未購買「移除廣告」，應用程式會透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。Google 可能使用廣告 ID 及類似的裝置資料來顯示及衡量廣告。你可以透過 Google Play 一次性購買以移除廣告。Google 的政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy。

購買

付款由 Google Play 處理。應用程式不會取得你的卡號或銀行資料，只會得知此 Google 帳戶是否已購買「移除廣告」。

我們不會收集的資料

PCamera 沒有帳戶。開發者沒有伺服器接收你的影片、密碼或聯絡人。

兒童

PCamera 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童。

聯絡

如有私隱問題，請使用 Google Play 上 PCamera 頁面所載的開發者電郵聯絡。
